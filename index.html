Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you prepare for your visit.

What treatments do you offer?

We provide a comprehensive range of services including general dentistry, dental implants, restorative care, preventive treatments, and cosmetic enhancements. A consultation is required to determine the best options for your specific needs.

How can I book an appointment?

You can easily book an appointment by calling us at +91 90032 22711, using the contact form on this website, or via WhatsApp.

Do you provide dental implant consultations?

Yes, we offer dedicated consultations to assess your suitability for dental implants, discuss the procedure, outline the expected timeline, and review associated costs transparently.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Please arrive a few minutes early for any necessary paperwork. Bring your medical history, a list of current medications, and any previous dental records or X-rays if available.

How long does a dental treatment take?

Treatment times vary widely depending on the procedure. A routine check-up and cleaning may take 30-45 minutes, while more complex treatments like implants will require scheduled sessions. We will outline time estimates during your consultation.

How often should I visit the dentist?

As a general guideline, we recommend a routine check-up and cleaning every six months. However, the frequency may vary based on your individual oral health status.
